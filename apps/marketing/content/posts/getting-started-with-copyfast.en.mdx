Running a Telegram or Discord community often involves the same small task every day: open a source channel, look for an announcement, copy it, and forward it to your own community. With several sources, switching between channels and checking for missed updates takes time.

CopyFast helps simplify that routine by monitoring source channels and syncing new messages to your community.

## Choose updates your members need

Product announcements, project updates, and community news are common starting points. Choose sources around what your members actually want to follow, and bring those updates into the community they already use.

Before getting started, confirm that you have permission to access and forward the content. Supported content depends on platform permissions and the service scope. Historical messages and member information require separate confirmation.

## Start with one source and one destination

Begin with a single source channel and a single destination. Decide which content you need, then check that a new message arrives where expected.

Review the text and links, confirm the destination, and check for duplicate forwarding. Once the workflow is working as expected, add more sources and adjust the monitoring frequency or pause it to match your operating schedule.

## Make more time for your community

Delivering a message is only part of community work. Explaining announcements, answering questions, and starting discussions still need your team's attention. CopyFast reduces repetitive forwarding so you can spend more time on those activities.
