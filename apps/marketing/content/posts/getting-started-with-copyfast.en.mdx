Running a Discord community often involves the same small task every day: open a source channel, look for an announcement, copy it, and forward it to your own community. With several sources, switching between channels and checking for missed updates takes time.

CopyFast helps simplify that routine by monitoring source channels and syncing new messages to your community.

## Choose updates your members need

Product announcements, project updates, and community news are common starting points. Choose sources around what your members actually want to follow, and bring those updates into the community they already use.

Before getting started, confirm that you have permission to access and forward the content. Supported content depends on platform permissions and the service scope. Historical messages and member information require separate confirmation.

## Start with one source and one destination

Begin with a single source channel and a single destination. Decide which content you need, then check that a new message arrives where expected.

Review the text and links, confirm the destination, and check for duplicate forwarding. Once the workflow is working as expected, add more sources and adjust the monitoring frequency or pause it to match your operating schedule.

## Make more time for your community

Delivering a message is only part of community work. Explaining announcements, answering questions, and starting discussions still need your team's attention. CopyFast reduces repetitive forwarding so you can spend more time on those activities.

## Adjust a desktop collection task

The desktop task detail page pairs each source channel with its destination. Use “编辑规则” (Edit rules) to change the check frequency, keywords, and content types, or “编辑对应” (Edit mappings) to update a destination and its masked Webhook URL. “仅保存，不运行” (Save without running) saves the configuration and pauses the task. “保存并运行” (Save and run) saves first, then starts using the new rules. A configured address does not confirm delivery; check messages and execution records for the result.

When exiting channel discovery, search by channel name or category and uncheck channels you do not need. Choose “Use selected channels and continue” to carry only checked channels into the next step. Clearing every channel returns to source settings.

During collection, CopyFast highlights the current visible Discord channel and shows its name and progress in the status overlay. New task logs include skip reasons and counts, such as messages older than the start time, duplicates, or keyword filtering. Older logs may not contain these details.

While a task is running, its status card updates the countdown every second and shows the scheduled next poll time. During collection it shows the active round; pausing stops the countdown, and retries show their own scheduled time.

For actionable Discord media nodes, the desktop collector attempts to obtain the original attachment link, downloads the file to a local cache, and uploads it with the message through a multipart Webhook. Confirmed uploads clear the cache; failed, rate-limited, or uncertain uploads retain it. The current per-message limit is 10 files and 20 MiB total. Missing originals stop the message with an error. Automatic link extraction, download, and local multipart upload have been verified with a real macOS image; live video and Windows collection still require separate verification.
