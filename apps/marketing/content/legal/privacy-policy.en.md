title: Privacy Policy
---

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how CopyFast handles personal information across our website, desktop clients and related services, including accounts, subscriptions, and Discord (DC) content cloning, monitoring, synchronization and forwarding. Processing depends on the features and settings you use.

### 1. Information we process

- **Account and authentication information:** Email, display name, account identifiers and information needed to authenticate you and maintain sessions. Third-party sign-in may supply basic profile information covered by your authorization.
- **Task and connection settings:** Source platforms, community and channel identifiers, destinations, monitoring frequency, task status and authorization information needed for enabled connections.
- **Messages and attachments:** When you enable the relevant features, message text, attachments or their links, message identifiers, timestamps, and author display names or identifiers needed to carry out your task. Features may process information on your device or through a server; this does not mean all data on your device is uploaded.
- **Orders and subscriptions:** Order identifiers, selected plans, transaction status and subscription status. Payment providers handle payment information under their policies. Do not send full card numbers or payment keys to support.
- **Access and operation records:** Access times, IP addresses, browser or device information, activity and error logs, and records needed for security, task operation and service quality analysis.
- **Support communications:** Questions, screenshots and troubleshooting materials you choose to submit. Remove unrelated personal information and credentials before sharing.

### 2. Purposes

We use information as needed to provide and perform the service, including account verification, configured synchronization tasks, subscription management, support, troubleshooting, abuse prevention and security. We also retain records where required for legal, tax and other statutory obligations.

Where applicable law requires consent, we obtain it. You may withdraw consent for optional processing through available controls or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing previously based on valid consent.

### 3. Source content and destination visibility

Configured messages may contain other community members’ personal information. Ensure you have the right to process and forward it, and provide notices or obtain permissions required by law. We process content according to your tasks; you choose sources, destinations and recipients.

After forwarding, destination members, administrators and the platform may access or redistribute the content. Stopping tasks or deleting a CopyFast account does not automatically remove copies on third-party platforms. Manage published content through the destination platform.

### 4. Cookies, local storage and analytics

We use cookies and browser local storage for authentication, language and privacy choices, and referral or campaign parameters used to associate visits with orders. Depending on deployment settings, some pages may enable analytics that process access, device and session information.

Where prior consent is legally required, nonessential technologies should reflect your choices. Browser settings let you manage or clear cookies and local storage. Blocking necessary storage may affect login, saved preferences or checkout.

### 5. Recipients

Necessary information may be provided to authentication, hosting, storage, payment, email and analytics providers, and platforms you connect, such as Discord. Information shared depends on the provider’s actual role and should be limited to what that role requires.

We do not sell your personal information. Necessary disclosures may be made to comply with law or valid legal process, protect legitimate rights or address security incidents. Any handling of information in a business transfer must comply with applicable law and required notices.

Third-party platforms and external services have their own privacy policies. Review them before connecting accounts or submitting information.

### 6. Retention and deletion

We retain information for the time needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, investigate security issues and meet legal obligations. Different information has different retention needs. When no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymized; backup copies follow backup rotation, except records that must legally be retained.

You may stop tasks, revoke connections or request deletion of your account and associated information. Identity verification may be needed to protect against unauthorized deletion. Data already received by third-party platforms is subject to their rules and your permissions there.

### 7. Security

We apply reasonable access controls, permission management and other safeguards appropriate to the information and risks. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. Protect account and connection credentials; revoke authorizations, update credentials and contact us if exposure occurs.

### 8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, obtain a copy of, correct or delete information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint. Contact us with your account and request scope. We respond within legally required periods and explain any lawful reason that prevents fulfillment.

If providers are outside your region, information may be processed internationally. We follow applicable requirements for notices, consent or other necessary safeguards.

### 9. Minors

The service is intended for users who meet legal eligibility requirements. Minors must comply with local age and guardian consent rules. Contact us if you believe we process a minor’s information contrary to those requirements.

### 10. Updates and contact

We date policy revisions here and provide reasonable notice of material changes to our main processing practices. Where renewed consent is required, we follow applicable law.

For privacy, deletion or data processing questions, contact CopyFast through the Discord channels on our [Contact page](/en/contact). Do not include passwords, session tokens or payment keys.
